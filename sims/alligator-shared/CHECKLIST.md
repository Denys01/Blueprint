   whole animal in frame at ~0.85 of the width, the tail curving back and down to a pointed tip near the floor.
2. Head 0.24 L: a long flat skull tapering to a broad rounded snout with a raised nostril bump; plates with amber lip
   lines along both jaws; never a round bulb.
3. Glass teeth #9CC9FF: straight conical, ~17 upper and ~15 lower a side, lit glass, never white or dark.
4. Eye 0.16 L back on a raised orbit on top of the skull: black glossy lens inside an amber bezel.
5. Back: raised ridged scutes in rows (4 across), the middle pair tallest, making a serrated crest; satin graphite.
6. Flank: curved transverse armour bands with gaps; steel and brass rings, pistons and hoses show in every gap.
7. Glass flank window mid-body (0.39–0.53 L): a rounded glass pane over brass gears and a steel cylinder.
8. Four sprawled legs (0.09 L): armoured upper limb, big round elbow/knee joint with an amber ring, steel pistons,
   feet with 4 steel toes and claws and glass webbing between them.
9. Tail 0.41 L: a chain of ~16 segments with banded armour and upright scute fins; the last third glass.
10. Materials: plates satin-metallic graphite; mechanism bright steel #8c939b and brass #B08A5A (kept light);
    amber only on edges, rims and joint rings, never dots.
11. Light: warm key from the left, cold blue rim from behind, visible floor shadow; bloom ≤ 0.15, threshold ≥ 1.2.
12. At 900 px wide it reads as a robotic alligator, not a lizard or a toy.
