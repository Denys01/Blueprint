# Checklist for one picture

The brief covers every build. The checklist covers one picture and is written before any code. 10 to 12 numbered lines, each something a stranger could check by looking at picture and still side by side.

Rules for writing lines:

- Say what the picture stands on: silhouette, pose, the one or two parts people will remember.
- Put a number in every line that can take one (a share of body length, a count, a thickness).
- Use "never" where the default would go wrong ("the dorsal fin is blueprint glass, never a dark shell").
- One line for the overall read at thumbnail size.

## Template

```
Picture: <file>          Measured: <proportions>   Detail share: <value>

1. Silhouette and pose: ...
2. The signature part: ...
3. ...
12. At 300 px wide it still reads as <subject>, not as a toy.
```

## Example: robotic hammerhead (Galeon NX-1)

```
Measured: hammer width 0.32 of body length, body height 0.18, dorsal fin height 0.14, tail 0.30.

1. A long shark swimming left to right, front 3/4 from slightly above, the hammer closest to the camera.
2. The hammer head is a wide flat bar, 0.32 of body length, with an amber-ringed black lens at each end.
3. The hammer's underside is open plates over a row of small gears, never one solid shell.
4. Gill slits are 5 parallel cuts on each side with amber edges and dark mechanism visible inside.
5. The dorsal fin is blueprint glass with 6 to 8 fine veins, never a dark shell.
6. Pectoral fins are glass, swept back about 35°, each on a visible dark hinge.
7. The flank is plates in 3 sizes with gaps; through the gaps a drive shaft and pistons run along the body.
8. The tail is a segmented stalk of 6 to 8 rings ending in a glass crescent fin.
9. Mouth under the hammer, slightly open, two rows of small blueprint teeth.
10. Amber only on lens bezels, gill edges, fin hinges and the moving part. Never dots.
11. Detail share at least 0.8 of the picture's.
12. At 300 px wide it reads as a hammerhead, not a submarine.
```
